# Category Guides

Apply only the section that matches the identified medium and subject. Multi-subject images may combine two sections. A section never imports features the image does not show. Across all sections: identity, exact age, ethnicity, place names, brand names and software names are written only when the image evidence supports them, otherwise describe the visible appearance or effect.

## General image

Cover subject content, medium style, color scheme, light, composition, materials, lens language, spatial layers, mood, quality and detail. The Chinese and English prompts carry identical meaning, with nothing added in either language.

## Portrait and people photography

Visible age impression and presentation, face shape, features, hair, makeup, expression, gaze direction, head angle, body orientation and pose, shoulder and neck line, hand and leg placement, center of weight, the body part nearest the camera and any perspective emphasis, wardrobe and accessories garment by garment, skin texture, and the person's relationship to the environment. When identity or age cannot be judged reliably, describe only the visible appearance.

## Photography in general

Natural, hard, soft, back or studio light, depth of field and focus behavior, wide natural or compressed perspective by effect, temperature, vintage, commercial-clean or filmic tonality, sharpness grain noise and motion blur, and the photographic kind: documentary, studio, lifestyle snapshot, fashion or film still. Specific camera parameters only on sufficient evidence, graded inferred even then.

## Product and commercial photography

Product category, shape, color, orientation and frame share, packaging structure, edges, buttons, ports and label positions described as areas not readable text, surface material, highlight and reflection behavior, roughness and transparency, shadow placement, props actually present such as water drops powder liquid ice or petals, background arrangement, and which visible structures carry the selling point.

## Landscape, nature and city

Environment type, season, weather, time of day read from the light, foreground midground background and horizon line, sky cloud water and air perspective, vegetation rock architecture street and light elements, perspective lines and building geometry, light direction and temperature change across the frame, depth, texture and mood. Never invent a specific place or landmark name from a vague resemblance.

## Architecture and interior

Space type and function, perspective direction and vanishing points, dominant lines, materials of structure furniture walls and floor, light sources including window light and light strips, spatial scale, order and tidiness, whether the space reads occupied or empty, and the connection between inside and outside.

## Illustration

Decouple before writing anything. Build two separate internal results:

- Content skeleton: what is drawn and where it sits, subject appearance and action, props, scene, composition positions, planes, occlusion and narrative relations. No style words allowed here.
- Style fingerprint: how the same content is drawn, only observable craft: medium and rendering method, line system, shape language, value and shading structure, color grammar, brushwork and surface texture, detail allocation, light and atmosphere handling, composition rhythm, post and print character.

Run the leak check: delete every content noun from the fingerprint. What remains must still define one coherent drawing method. A fingerprint item that only repeats a subject or a plot point moves back to the skeleton. One primary style direction rules the medium, line and shading logic, with at most two supporting techniques for color, texture or finish. Resolve conflicts by keeping the side with stronger evidence and larger coverage: flat vector against heavy oil impasto, no outline against thick closed outline, hard cel layers against continuous watercolor bleed, minimal detail against dense realistic texture, matte paper against glossy plastic reflection, loose hand edges against mechanical uniform line weight. For illustration anchors, choose 3 to 5 with at least two from the fingerprint: one medium or rendering anchor, one line shape or shading anchor, one color or light anchor, one texture or detail-allocation anchor. In the prompt, roughly 35 to 45 percent describes the fingerprint and anchors, 20 to 30 percent the subject, 15 to 20 percent composition view light and space, the rest background narrative mood post and medium boundary, with the style anchors inside the first third.

## 3D render

Realistic, semi-realistic, cartoon, clay, designer-toy or minimal kind, model proportions and edge softness, detail precision, material set including matte metal glass acrylic resin PVC and ceramic, reflection refraction roughness subsurface scattering and PBR behavior, three-point soft rim volumetric or ambient lighting, shadow softness and contact shadows, anti-aliasing and post glow. Renderer or software names only on sufficient evidence or user instruction.

## IP character, chibi and designer toy

Style kind, head-to-body proportion, silhouette, features, expression, hair, outfit and ornaments, action, matte resin PVC ceramic or clay texture, soft light, plain background, display stand or product presentation, full-body completeness and detail density. The output describes visible features and design language, never the protected name.

## Animal

Species when reliably identifiable, otherwise visible build and appearance only. Body size, coat feather or scale color, pose, action, eyes, fur feather scale skin or claw texture, relation to environment and other subjects, rim light and mood.

## Food

Food kind and main ingredients, shape, color, plating, frame share, gloss sauce steam sear moisture cream powder frosting stretch or drip, utensils and table material, light, shadow, depth of field, camera angle, and which colors gloss textures and temperature cues create the appetite appeal.

## Vehicle

Vehicle type, viewing angle, body material and reflections, light strips and highlight lines, still or moving state, road sky city or nature setting, speed lines dust splash or motion blur only when present, perspective and frame share. Exclude readable logos, plates and numbers by default.

## Plant and flower

Species when reliably identifiable, otherwise visible form. Color, count, growth state, petal leaf pollen dew and texture detail, light passing through petals or leaves, occlusion, blur and background relation, and the concrete sources of any soft fresh romantic or quiet mood.

## Typography, logo, poster and graphic design

Style kind such as modern vintage cyber handwritten minimal or decorative, letterform width slant rounding geometry and proportion, stroke weight and serif structure, cuts connections breaks and negative space, spacing leading hierarchy and layout logic, outline glow gradient emboss shadow and dimensional effects, material such as metal frosted glass neon or paper, palette and composition. When the goal is the layout rather than the content, write reserved text areas. When text is only a picture element, exclude readable text. A design that is itself the subject may be described by its letterform and structure, still without relying on a protected name.

## Abstract and mixed subjects

Basic geometry, curves, color blocks, texture, repetition pattern, hierarchy, rhythm, balance, direction, transparency layering, glow, gradients and materials, plus distances occlusion proportions and narrative relations between subjects. Combine only the sections that actually apply.
